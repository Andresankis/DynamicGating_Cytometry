Flow cytometry was performed on a Beckman Coulter Navios EX instrument, which features three lasers (405, 488, and 638 nm), two light scattering detectors, and ten fluorescence detectors. To monitor ligand-receptor binding kinetics, reactions were started by introducing a fluorescently labeled antibody into the cell suspension. Aliquots (10 µL) were withdrawn at specific intervals for each receptor (e.g., CD14 at 1, 6, 12... min; CD8 at 1, 6, 12... min, etc.) and transferred into 600 µL of PBS to stop the reaction. These quenched samples were subsequently analyzed by flow cytometry. Cell concentration was quantified in each experiment through the addition of Flow-Count fluorospheres. The entire procedure was carried out at room temperature.
